# V11 PWS observation lead: research and settlement integration

`v11/pws_lead.py` archives paired next-observation research. Its first-received
report scores remain descriptive DEVELOPMENT data, not independently supported
next-published labels, market lead, contract payouts or sale proceeds. See
`V11_PWS_LEAD.md` for the research provenance and measurement limits.

`v11/pws_admission.py` now joins that immutable observation calculation to two
separate protected strategy/model admissions: NEXT_OFFICIAL_OBSERVATION and
FINAL_CONTRACT_PAYOUT. Both require exact station, rule, lifecycle and common
official/PWS source leases. Account, season, time-of-day and code/config identity
must match. Each model retains its own scope, bundle, epoch, capability review
and expiry. The observation prediction is recomputed with the approved bundle;
research hashes alone do not grant approval. This join remains a revocable data
pin and requires separate qualified economics before any paper reservation.

The PWS branch in `v11/strategy_pipeline.py` uses the payout champion with the
exact official running extreme and complete unresolved contract-day coverage.
It computes SETTLEMENT value against depth and costs. It never substitutes the
next-observation vector or a presumed confirmation price jump for contract value.
REPRICING remains unavailable without a separately supported net-exit model.
Current vacuous conservative payout bounds reject entry even when the synthetic
protected-review fixtures pass. No actual eligible strategy is claimed.

The common paper coordinator requires the paired pin for every PWS-attributed
proposal. Both scopes revalidate before reservation and paper submission-state
transition. The stricter size reduction and earliest model/source/lead-window
expiry apply. Official arrivals, PWS/model revisions and model or station demotion
invalidate the old thesis. There is no extra PWS budget: existing desired-position
netting, cash reservations, exact event outcomes and correlated ceilings apply.

Exact archived book freshness and latest source revision are rechecked before
reservation and submission-state transition. Book and source heads join the
account transaction's compare-and-swap guards. A changed book requires a new
valuation; an official update requires a new thesis. Failure preserves existing
reservations and permits cancellation requests/reconciliation. These checks do
not claim a live CLOB fetch or actual submission: this coordinator has no order
transport, account credentials or financial authority.

Tests cover paired target identity, separate model demotion, strict sensor-policy
expiry, changed release/account, missing joins, receipt races, duplicate-exposure
netting, book supersession and retained reservations. Downstream positive-EV
fixtures are explicitly synthetic and exercise account mechanics only. Protected
custody/reviews are fixture substitutes, not owner commissioning or independent
review. Raw-source adapters, actual calibrated/approved champions, independent
lead/label evidence, transport and runtime acceptance remain unfinished.
